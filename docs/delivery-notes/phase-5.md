## What was delivered

Phase 5 focused on completing the event booking flow and giving coordinators and
organizers better visibility and control over events.

### For attendees

Attendees can now book an event or be placed on its waitlist when seats are full.
They can leave a waitlist, filter events by date, and view their personal attendance
history. Capacity-capped reservations and automatic waitlist promotion connect these
actions into one complete booking experience.

### For coordinators and organizers

Coordinators can search venues by minimum capacity, see summary statistics on the
event dashboard, remove organizer assignments, and assign door organizers to events.
These changes make it easier to prepare events and manage the people responsible for
them.

Together, the Phase 5 work moved the system beyond basic reservations toward a
fuller event operations workflow.
